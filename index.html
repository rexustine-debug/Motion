1 Introduction#

Motion Studio AI (hereinafter referred to as "the Organisers", "we", "our" or "us") recognises the importance of protecting the privacy, personal information and digital rights of individuals who register for or participate in the FREE AI MASTERCLASS: CREATING AI VIDEOS FOR BRANDS AND BUSINESSES (hereinafter referred to as "the Programme").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, receive, use, process, store, disclose, retain and protect personal information provided by prospective participants, registered participants, parents and legal guardians in connection with the Programme.

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable regulatory requirements.

By registering for or participating in the Programme, participants acknowledge that they have been provided with this Privacy Policy and understand how their personal information may be processed as described herein.

Where a participant is a minor, the applicable parental or legal guardian consent requirements shall also apply in accordance with our Parental Consent and Child Participation Policy and applicable law.

2 Personal Information We Collect#

Depending on the registration process and the participant's circumstances, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

A. Identification And Contact Information

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone number.
  • WhatsApp contact information.
  • Country of residence.
  • Educational institution or professional affiliation, where applicable.

B. Participant And Registration Information

  • Age or age category.
  • Participant classification or category.
  • Previous experience with artificial intelligence tools.
  • Learning objectives and expectations.
  • Areas of interest.
  • Identified learning challenges.
  • Availability and level of commitment.
  • Other information voluntarily supplied through registration forms.

C. Parental Or Guardian Information

Where a participant is below the age of eighteen (18), we may collect information necessary to establish parental or legal guardian authorisation, including:

  • Parent or legal guardian's full name.
  • Relationship to the minor.
  • Parent or legal guardian's telephone number.
  • Email address.
  • Confirmation of parental or legal guardian authorisation.
  • Information reasonably necessary to verify the authenticity of such authorisation.

D. Digital And Technical Information

Where necessary for registration, programme administration, security or communication, we may process limited technical information, including:

  • Telegram username or account information.
  • Platform-generated identifiers.
  • Registration timestamps.
  • Submission records.
  • Technical information made available through the platforms used to administer the Programme.

We will endeavour to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy.

3 Purposes of Collection and Processing#

Personal information collected in connection with the Programme may be processed for the following purposes:

A. Registration And Participation

  • Receiving and processing registration applications.
  • Confirming participant eligibility.
  • Maintaining registration records.
  • Communicating registration outcomes.
  • Managing access to the Programme and associated learning platforms.

B. Programme Administration

  • Sending programme announcements and updates.
  • Communicating schedules, instructions and learning materials.
  • Managing participant enquiries.
  • Providing technical and administrative assistance.
  • Monitoring participation and programme delivery.

C. Child Protection And Parental Authorisation

  • Establishing whether parental or legal guardian consent is required.
  • Receiving and documenting parental or guardian authorisation.
  • Conducting appropriate age and consent verification.
  • Addressing safeguarding concerns.
  • Protecting the interests and welfare of minor participants.

D. Evaluation And Improvement

  • Assessing programme participation and effectiveness.
  • Understanding participant learning needs.
  • Improving future educational programmes.
  • Preparing internal reports and programme statistics.

Where possible, statistical reporting and programme evaluation will use aggregated or anonymised information.

4 Lawful Basis for Processing#

We will process personal information only where an appropriate lawful basis exists under applicable data protection legislation.

Depending on the nature and circumstances of the processing, these lawful bases may include:

  • The consent of the data subject.
  • Consent of a parent or legally authorised guardian where required for the processing of a child's personal information.
  • Processing necessary for the performance of arrangements connected with participation in the Programme.
  • Compliance with legal obligations.
  • Legitimate interests, where permitted by law and where such interests do not override the rights and freedoms of the affected individual.

Where processing is based on consent, the individual or legally authorised representative, as applicable, may withdraw that consent subject to applicable legal requirements.

Withdrawal of consent shall not invalidate processing lawfully undertaken before the withdrawal.

5 Processing of Children's Personal Information#

We recognise that individuals below the age of eighteen (18) require particular consideration and protection when their personal information is collected or processed.

Where a participant is a minor, we will apply appropriate safeguards in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable child protection and data protection requirements.

Where consent is the applicable lawful basis for processing a minor's personal information, the Organisers shall obtain the consent of the minor's parent or legally authorised guardian as required by law.

We may apply appropriate mechanisms to establish the minor's age and verify the identity, relationship and authority of the person providing parental or guardian consent.

We will endeavour to:

  • Limit the collection of children's personal information to what is reasonably necessary.
  • Use children's personal information only for legitimate and disclosed purposes.
  • Restrict access to children's information to authorised individuals.
  • Apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards.
  • Avoid unnecessary disclosure of children's personal information.
  • Respond appropriately to requests from parents or legal guardians concerning their children's information.

The participation of a minor shall be subject to the requirements contained in our Parental Consent and Child Participation Policy.

Nothing in this Policy shall be interpreted as dispensing with any parental consent or verification requirement imposed by applicable law.

6 Marketing, Promotional Communications and Remarketing#

6.1 Marketing and Promotional Rights#

The Organisers may, from time to time, communicate marketing, promotional, informational and commercial content to participants concerning future educational programmes, AI masterclasses, training opportunities, events, resources, products, services, partnerships, initiatives and other activities associated with the Organisers or their legitimate business and programme interests.

6.2 Communication Channels#

Such marketing and promotional communications may be delivered through various communication channels, including but not limited to electronic mail (email), WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, telephone communications, social media platforms and other existing or subsequently developed digital communication channels. The Organisers shall not be restricted to any single communication channel, provided that the relevant communication and processing activities comply with applicable data protection and electronic communications laws.

6.3 Consent to Marketing and Promotional Communications#

Participation in the Programme does not automatically constitute consent to receive marketing or promotional communications. The Organisers shall obtain separate and voluntary consent from participants before using their personal information for direct marketing purposes where such consent is required by applicable law.

By affirmatively providing marketing consent through the designated registration or consent mechanism, participants authorise the Organisers to use their voluntarily supplied contact information for the marketing, promotional and informational purposes described in this Section.

6.4 Use of Information for Remarketing#

Subject to applicable law and the consent provided, the Organisers may retain and use relevant participant contact information for future marketing, audience engagement, promotional outreach and remarketing activities. Such activities may include communicating information about subsequent programmes, inviting previous participants to future events, sharing relevant opportunities and maintaining relationships with individuals who have expressed an interest in the Organisers' activities.

The Organisers shall not interpret consent for marketing communications as unrestricted permission to process personal information for unrelated purposes.

6.5 Voluntary Nature of Marketing Consent#

Marketing consent is entirely voluntary and is not a condition for registration, admission or participation in the Programme. Participants who decline marketing consent shall not be disadvantaged or denied access to the Programme solely because they have declined such consent.

6.6 Withdrawal of Marketing Consent and Right to Object#

Participants may withdraw their marketing consent or object to the processing of their personal information for direct marketing purposes at any time by using an available unsubscribe mechanism or contacting the Organisers through the designated privacy communication channel.

Upon receipt of a valid withdrawal or objection, the Organisers shall cease the relevant direct marketing activities in accordance with applicable law.

6.7 Marketing Communications Involving Minors#

Where a participant is below the age of eighteen (18), any processing of personal information for marketing or promotional purposes shall be subject to applicable child data protection requirements and any necessary parental or legal guardian authorisation.

Parental or guardian consent to a minor's participation in the Programme shall not automatically constitute consent to marketing or promotional communications directed to that minor.

6.8 Compliance with Applicable Law#

All marketing, promotional and remarketing activities undertaken by the Organisers shall be conducted in accordance with applicable data protection, privacy and electronic communications laws. Nothing in this Section shall be interpreted as authorising any marketing, promotional or remarketing activity that is inconsistent with applicable law.

7 Photography, Video Recordings and Publicity#

The Programme may involve online presentations, demonstrations, interactive sessions or other activities that could involve photographs, screenshots, audio recordings or video recordings.

Where the Organisers intend to record or publicly distribute identifiable images, voices, testimonials or other personal representations of participants for publicity, promotional or publication purposes, appropriate permission shall be obtained separately where required.

Registration for or participation in the Programme shall not, by itself, constitute permission for the Organisers to use a participant's photograph, video, voice or testimonial for promotional purposes.

Where a minor is concerned, appropriate parental or legal guardian authorisation shall be obtained in accordance with applicable law.

Participants shall not be required to grant publicity permission as a condition of registration unless a specific activity genuinely requires it and the relevant conditions have been clearly communicated.

8 Disclosure and Sharing of Personal Information#

The Organisers do not sell participants' personal information.

However, personal information may be disclosed or made accessible to third parties where reasonably necessary for the administration, delivery, security or lawful operation of the Programme.

Such third parties may include:

  • Google services, including Google Forms and Google Sheets, used for registration and record management.
  • Telegram and associated communication services used for participant communication and programme administration.
  • Technology service providers supporting the operation of the Programme.
  • Authorised programme administrators and personnel.
  • Professional advisers, regulators or public authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Where third-party service providers process personal information on our behalf, we will endeavour to ensure that appropriate contractual, technical and organisational safeguards are maintained.

We will not knowingly disclose personal information to unrelated third parties for their independent marketing purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

9 International Transfers of Personal Information#

Some digital platforms and technology service providers used in administering the Programme may process or store information in locations outside Nigeria.

Where personal information is transferred outside Nigeria, the Organisers shall take appropriate steps to ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection legislation.

Such steps may include relying on an applicable lawful transfer mechanism and implementing appropriate safeguards where required.

Participants acknowledge that third-party digital platforms may operate infrastructure in different countries and may therefore process information in accordance with their respective legal and contractual obligations.

10 Data Security and Confidentiality#

The Organisers shall take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration, disclosure, destruction or other unlawful processing.

These measures may include:

  • Restricting access to registration records.
  • Limiting access to authorised programme personnel.
  • Applying appropriate account and platform security measures.
  • Maintaining reasonable confidentiality practices.
  • Reviewing access to personal information where necessary.
  • Taking appropriate action where a suspected data security incident occurs.

Notwithstanding these measures, no electronic transmission, online platform, database or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Participants should therefore avoid submitting passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information unless specifically required through a lawful and appropriately secured process.

11 Data Retention#

Personal information shall be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain legitimate programme records or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

When personal information is no longer required, the Organisers shall take reasonable steps to delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

The specific retention periods applicable to different categories of information may vary depending on their purpose and legal requirements.

Where a participant withdraws consent, the Organisers shall assess whether the relevant information should be deleted, restricted or retained on another lawful basis.

Subject to applicable law, individuals whose personal information is processed in connection with the Programme may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally applicable.
  • Request restriction of processing in appropriate circumstances.

  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Where a participant is a minor, a parent or legally authorised guardian may exercise applicable rights on the minor's behalf, subject to the law and appropriate verification of authority.

Requests concerning personal information may be submitted using the contact details provided in this Policy.

The Programme may rely on external platforms and services to facilitate registration, communication, learning activities and programme administration.

1 Purpose of This Agreement#

This Parental/Guardian Consent and Participation Authorisation Agreement ("Agreement") is intended to obtain the informed permission of a parent or legally recognised guardian for a participant who is below the age of eighteen (18) years ("Minor") to register for and participate in the Motion AI Masterclass ("Programme").

The Programme may involve educational activities, demonstrations, practical exercises, digital tools, artificial intelligence applications, online learning platforms, communication channels and other related activities.

This Agreement is separate from the Programme's Privacy Policy. By providing consent, the parent or guardian acknowledges that they have had the opportunity to review the Privacy Policy.

2 Eligibility and Age Requirement#

Motion AI Masterclass welcomes participants across different age groups.

There is no additional minimum age specified for minors under this Agreement. However, every participant aged seventeen (17) years and below must have the consent and authorisation of a parent or legally recognised guardian before participating in the Programme.

Registration by a minor does not constitute parental consent.

Motion reserves the right to request reasonable evidence of age, parental identity or guardianship where necessary to verify the validity of the consent provided.

a.I am the parent or legally recognised guardian of the minor identified in the registration form.

b.I have the legal authority to grant permission for the minor to participate in the Motion AI Masterclass.

c.I have read and understood the nature, purpose and general activities of the Programme.

d.I voluntarily authorise the minor to register for and participate in the Programme, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

e.I understand that the Programme may involve the use of artificial intelligence tools, digital platforms, online services and third-party applications.

f.I will take reasonable steps to ensure that the minor complies with applicable platform age restrictions, safety requirements and the instructions communicated by the Programme organisers.

4 Artificial Intelligence Tools and Third-Party Platforms#

I acknowledge that the Programme may introduce or demonstrate third-party artificial intelligence tools, software, websites and digital platforms.

I understand that:

a.Some third-party platforms may impose their own minimum age requirements, parental permission requirements or geographical restrictions.

c.The minor must not falsify their age, misrepresent their identity or bypass the age restrictions or safety mechanisms of any third-party platform.

d.Where a platform requires a parent or guardian to create, supervise or approve an account, that requirement remains the responsibility of the parent or guardian.

f.The parent or guardian is encouraged to supervise the minor's use of external AI tools and digital services introduced during the Programme.
